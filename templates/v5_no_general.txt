RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
1.	Provide advice, guidance and consultancy to researchers and other stakeholders on research data management (RDM) throughout the research data lifecycle.
2.	Support researchers in developing, writing and implementing data management plans (DMPs), including advising on funder and institutional requirements.
3.	Develop, review and maintain institutional policies, procedures, standards and guidelines for data management, data quality, data protection and data governance.
4.	Design, develop and deliver training, workshops, presentations and resources on data management and related topics (e.g. RDM, FAIR, open science, data protection).
5.	Promote and support the adoption and implementation of FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) data principles.
6.	Promote and support open science, open research and open data-sharing practices, including advocacy for a cultural shift towards openness.
7.	Manage and support data curation activities, including cleaning, structuring and preparing data and datasets for use, storage or publication.
8.	Create, apply and maintain metadata and documentation standards to support data discovery, quality, interoperability and reuse.
9.	Support and advise on data access requests, data submissions, and data-sharing agreements with internal and external data providers or partners.
10.	Support compliance with data protection and information governance requirements (e.g. GDPR), including support with Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) and related legal/ethical documentation.
11.	Contribute to the design, implementation and maintenance of data infrastructure, systems and data stewardship tools (e.g. repositories, catalogues, portals, platforms).
12.	Undertake data audits, quality checks and validation processes to monitor and maintain data integrity.
13.	Facilitate data linkage, integration and coordinated access to datasets across internal teams, systems or external organisations.
14.	Develop, maintain and improve data catalogues, repositories, registers or portals, and support the discoverability, dissemination and reuse of data related outputs.
15.	Contribute specialist input to research proposals, business cases, funding applications, or ethics applications involving the creation, collection or management of data.
16.	Represent the organisation in relevant external networks, working groups, conferences and community initiatives related to data management and stewardship.
17.	Provide technical guidance, troubleshooting and support for research data systems and infrastructure, including liaising with specialist teams where necessary.
18.	Support the planning and coordination of data storage, sharing, retention, archiving and long-term preservation of data.
19.	Contribute to the development and dissemination of best practice, standards and knowledge-sharing in data management, through publications, technical reports, presentations or community fora.
20.	Support the development of organisational data strategy, roadmaps, or maturity assessments, including systematic audits of the scale and nature of datasets.
21.	Coordinate, supervise or support the work of other data stewards or staff engaged in data management activities.
22.	Keep up to date with emerging technologies, tools, standards, regulations and best practice in data management and stewardship, and apply this awareness to the role.
23.	Respond to enquiries from a range of users (from students to senior staff) regarding data, data management or data-related systems.
24.	Manage or support the acquisition, ingestion and provisioning of new datasets, including assessing utility, quality and completeness of incoming data/content.
25.	Ensure appropriate governance and compliance is followed for access to sensitive or domain-specific regulated data in line with relevant sector standards.
26.	Support public, patient or wider stakeholder engagement activities that promote transparent, secure and trustworthy use of data.
27.	Mint and maintain Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs) or similar persistent identifiers, ensuring compliance with relevant minimum metadata requirements.
